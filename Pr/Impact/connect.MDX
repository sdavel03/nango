                  -H "Provider-Config-Key: <INTEGRATION-ID>" \

                  -H "Accept: application/json"

  <Tab title="🚨 API gotchas">
      - Impact.com uses HTTP Basic auth. The **Account SID** is the username and the **Auth Token** is the password.
      - The Account SID also appears in the path of nearly every endpoint (`/Advertisers/{AccountSid}/...`), so it is needed twice: once as the credential and once in the request URL.
      - The API defaults to XML. Send `Accept: application/json` on every request or you will get XML back.
      - Responses are paginated with `Page` and `PageSize`, and the page count is returned in the body rather than in a header.
      - Actions are provisional: a transaction can move between `PENDING`, `APPROVED` and `REVERSED` for weeks after the sale, so the same date range pulled twice can legitimately differ.

# Overview

To authenticate with Impact.com, you need:
1. **Account SID** - the identifier for your Impact.com account. It is also part of the URL of most API endpoints.
2. **Auth Token** - the secret paired with that Account SID.

This guide will walk you through finding both within Impact.com.

### Prerequisites:

- You must have an Impact.com account with access to Settings.

#### Step 1: Finding Your Account SID and Auth Token
1. Sign in to [Impact.com](https://app.impact.com/).
2. Open **Settings** from the left-hand menu.
3. Choose **API** (listed under Technical Settings).
4. Your **Account SID** and **Auth Token** are both shown on this page.
5. Copy them.

<Tip>Impact.com uses Basic auth: the Account SID is the username and the Auth Token is the password. You do not need to combine or encode them yourself.</Tip>

#### Step 2: Enter credentials in the Connect UI

Once you have both values:
1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with Impact.com.
2. Enter your **Account SID** and your **Auth Token** in the designated fields.
3. Submit the form, and you should be successfully authenticated.

You are now connected to Impact.com.
